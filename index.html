Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 9, 2026

If Pray (“we,” “our,” or “us”) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use the If Pray app.

Information We Collect

If Pray may collect information you provide, such as your name, email address, prayer requests, journal entries, and content you choose to save.

Some features may also use your microphone for voice-based prayer and Echo Prayer.

How We Use Information

We use your information to:

  • Provide and personalize prayer features
  • Generate AI-assisted prayers
  • Save your prayer journal and preferences
  • Provide community and prayer-chain features
  • Send reminders and notifications
  • Improve and secure the app

AI Features

If Pray uses AI to generate personalized prayers and Scripture-related content. Information you enter into AI features may be processed to provide these services.

AI-generated content is for spiritual support and does not replace personal prayer, pastoral guidance, or professional advice.

Sharing Information

We do not sell your personal information.

We may use trusted third-party services for hosting, authentication, AI, analytics, notifications, and payments. These services only receive information necessary to provide their services.

Your Choices

You may request access to or deletion of your personal information by contacting us.

Children's Privacy

If Pray is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children without appropriate parental or guardian involvement.

Security

We use reasonable measures to protect your information, but no online service can guarantee complete security.

Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page.
